# Phase 02 — Quest Core: requirement gate (G0) and acceptance criteria

Status: implemented 2026-09-07 on branch `phase-02-quest-core`; verified by the automated suites
named under each criterion (`apps/api/test/integration/quests.int.test.ts` = INT,
`apps/api/test/e2e/quest-journey.e2e.test.ts` = E2E, `apps/api/test/integration/database.int.test.ts`
= DB, unit suites = UNIT).

## User value

A person can write a Quest on a phone, have it checked against the safety policy, publish it, and
have other people find it, accept it, start it and declare that they have done it. A Quest is a
governed executable challenge rather than a post: it becomes visible only through an explicit,
audited transition that a safety decision about _that exact content_ authorises, and it leaves
visibility again the moment that decision stops applying. Everything later — evidence
verification, XP, ranking, social distribution — hangs off a Quest id that never changes and a
published version that a participant's terms are frozen against.

## Success metrics (instrumented through the MetricsPort, dashboards in the release gate)

- Draft → published within 7 days ≥ 50 % of drafts (`quest.core.drafted`, `quest.core.published`).
- Publish attempts blocked by the gate ≤ 25 % after the first attempt (`quest.core.publish_blocked`).
- Accept → start ≥ 70 %, start → completion requested ≥ 50 % (`quest.participation.accepted`,
  `.started`, `.completion_requested`).
- Attempts lost to an elapsed window ≤ 20 % of starts (`quest.participation.expired`).
- Safety outcome mix is watched, not targeted: `quest.core.assessed{state}` is the signal that the
  lexicon has drifted in either direction.

## Out of scope (explicitly not built)

XP, badges, levels, streaks and any reward; leaderboards; followers and the social graph; crews;
the feed; World Quest; the creator platform and brand monetisation; the AI Quest Generator and any
AI-produced safety decision; recommendation, ranking and personalisation (discovery is deliberately
newest-first and unranked); advanced Proof AI; and evidence verification of any kind.

Participation deliberately ends at `COMPLETION_REQUESTED`. A participant declares completion and
the attempt stops there: `PARTICIPATION_TRANSITIONS.COMPLETION_REQUESTED` is empty, no evidence is
collected, nothing is verified and nothing is awarded. Evidence and verification arrive in a later
phase, and the E2E journey asserts on the field names of a completed attempt that no reward or
verification field has appeared early.

Also absent by design: precise location (a Quest carries a country code and a label, no
coordinates), a moderation queue with cases and appeals (staff have suspend/reinstate and nothing
more), and any Quest media (the Quest context stores no object-storage keys, which is why its
erasure contributor returns none).

## Notes on two rules that are easy to misread

**The published age band, not the draft one.** `quest.eligibility` is editable draft content;
`quest.published_minimum_age_band` is what the Quest actually went live with, after any tightening
the safety decision imposed. Read access, discovery filtering, acceptance and the card all use the
published band. Nothing in Phase 02 lets an edit to the draft loosen a gate that is already live —
a safety-relevant edit unpublishes the Quest instead.

**404, not 403.** A Quest the caller may not see must not be distinguishable from one that does not
exist, including on the acceptance path: a 403 explaining "not eligible: QUEST_NOT_PUBLISHED" would
confirm someone else's draft. Only refusals about a Quest the caller may legitimately see carry
reasons.
